The leader listened to Tim and Sally. He said, "You are right. We should not display bad pictures in our town. Let's change it." So, the leader asked a painter to make a new, beautiful picture for the billboard. The people in the town were happy to see the new picture. Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a lazy frog named Lily. She liked to sit on her big green leaf and watch the fish swim by. One sunny day, a little bug flew by and asked, "Why are you so lazy, Lily?"

Lily just shrugged and said, "I like being lazy. It's fun to watch the fish and enjoy the sun." The little bug didn't understand and flew away.

The next day, a big rain came. All the other frogs were busy swimming and playing in the water. But Lily just sat on her leaf, being lazy. A small fish swam up to her and said, "Come play with us, Lily!"

Lily shrugged again and said, "No, thank you. I like being lazy on my leaf." The small fish swam away, sad that Lily didn't want to play.

In the end, Lily stayed lazy on her leaf, watching the world go by. She liked her simple, lazy life, and that was okay.
